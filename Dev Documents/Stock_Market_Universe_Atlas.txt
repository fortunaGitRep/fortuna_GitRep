STOCK MARKET UNIVERSE
│
├── 01 MARKET FOUNDATIONS  (the concepts everything else sits on)
│   ├── What a market is
│   │   ├── Stock market            — place where ownership of companies is traded
│   │   ├── Primary market          — company sells NEW shares to raise money (IPO)
│   │   ├── Secondary market        — investors trade EXISTING shares with each other
│   │   └── Price discovery         — buyers vs sellers continuously set the price
│   ├── Core terms
│   │   ├── Share / equity          — a slice of ownership in a company
│   │   ├── Market capitalisation   — price × total shares = company's market value
│   │   ├── Liquidity               — how easily you can buy/sell without moving price
│   │   ├── Bid / Ask / Spread      — best buy price, best sell price, gap between them
│   │   ├── Volume                  — how many shares changed hands
│   │   └── Volatility              — how much and how fast price swings
│   ├── Market cap buckets (SEBI definition)
│   │   ├── Large cap               — top 100 companies by market cap (stable)
│   │   ├── Mid cap                 — ranks 101–250 (growth + moderate risk)
│   │   └── Small cap               — rank 251 onwards (high growth, high risk)
│   ├── Market phases
│   │   ├── Bull market             — sustained rise, optimism
│   │   ├── Bear market             — sustained fall (~20%+), pessimism
│   │   ├── Correction              — 10–20% pullback inside a trend
│   │   └── Sideways / range        — no clear direction, chops between levels
│   └── Learning resources
│       ├── Zerodha Varsity          — best free India-specific market course
│       ├── NSE Academy / NISM       — exchange- and regulator-backed learning
│       └── Investopedia             — fastest lookup for any market term
│
├── 02 MARKET PARTICIPANTS  (who is on the other side of your trade)
│   ├── Retail
│   │   ├── Retail investors         — individuals, long-term, small ticket size
│   │   └── Retail traders           — individuals, short-term, often in F&O
│   ├── Institutions
│   │   ├── FIIs / FPIs              — foreign money; big driver of Indian index moves
│   │   ├── DIIs                     — domestic MFs, insurers, pension funds (SIP flows)
│   │   ├── Mutual funds (AMCs)      — pool retail money into managed portfolios
│   │   └── Insurance / EPFO / NPS   — very long-horizon, steady buyers
│   ├── Professional / speculative
│   │   ├── Proprietary traders      — trade the firm's own capital
│   │   ├── HFT / market makers      — provide liquidity, earn the spread
│   │   ├── Hedge funds / AIFs       — complex strategies, accredited investors
│   │   └── PMS                      — managed portfolio for HNIs (₹50L minimum)
│   ├── Insiders
│   │   ├── Promoters                — founders/controlling group; holding % is a signal
│   │   └── Insiders / KMPs          — management; trades are disclosed and restricted
│   └── Data to track them
│       ├── FII/DII daily flow data  — NSE publishes cash-market buy/sell daily
│       ├── Shareholding pattern     — quarterly promoter/FII/DII/retail split
│       └── Bulk / block deals       — large single trades, disclosed same day
│
├── 03 REGULATORS & MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE  (the plumbing nobody sees)
│   ├── Regulators
│   │   ├── SEBI                     — India's securities market regulator
│   │   ├── RBI                      — rates, currency, banks (moves markets indirectly)
│   │   ├── SEC (US)                 — US securities regulator
│   │   └── IFSCA                    — regulator for GIFT City international markets
│   ├── Exchanges (India)
│   │   ├── NSE                      — dominant in equity cash + almost all F&O volume
│   │   ├── BSE                      — oldest in Asia; SENSEX; growing F&O share
│   │   ├── MCX                      — commodity derivatives (gold, crude, metals)
│   │   └── NSE IX (GIFT City)       — GIFT Nifty, trades nearly 24 hours in USD
│   ├── Clearing corporations
│   │   ├── NSE Clearing (NCL)       — guarantees settlement of NSE trades
│   │   └── ICCL                     — clearing for BSE
│   ├── Depositories
│   │   ├── NSDL                     — holds shares electronically (demat)
│   │   └── CDSL                     — the other depository; most discount brokers use it
│   └── Market-wide safety systems
│       ├── Circuit breakers         — index-wide halt at 10/15/20% moves
│       ├── Price bands              — per-stock daily up/down limits
│       └── Settlement guarantee fund — protects against a broker defaulting
│
├── 04 EQUITY INSTRUMENTS  (what you can actually own)
│   ├── Shares
│   │   ├── Equity shares            — ordinary ownership + voting rights
│   │   ├── Preference shares        — fixed dividend priority, usually no vote
│   │   └── DVRs                     — differential voting rights shares
│   ├── Corporate actions
│   │   ├── Dividend                 — cash paid out of profits
│   │   ├── Bonus issue              — free shares; price adjusts, value unchanged
│   │   ├── Stock split              — more shares at lower price, value unchanged
│   │   ├── Rights issue             — existing holders offered new shares at a discount
│   │   ├── Buyback                  — company buys back its own shares
│   │   ├── Merger / demerger        — companies combine or split off a business
│   │   └── Delisting                — stock removed from the exchange
│   ├── Key dates
│   │   ├── Record date              — who's on the books gets the benefit
│   │   └── Ex-date                  — buy on/after this and you DON'T get it
│   └── Other listed equity-like
│       ├── REITs                    — own rent-earning real estate, pay out income
│       ├── InvITs                   — own infra assets (roads, power lines)
│       └── SME platform stocks      — small companies (NSE Emerge / BSE SME), thin liquidity
│
├── 05 DERIVATIVES  (contracts whose value comes from something else)
│   ├── Core types
│   │   ├── Futures                  — obligation to buy/sell at a fixed price on expiry
│   │   ├── Options                  — right (not obligation) to buy/sell
│   │   │   ├── Call (CE)            — profits when price goes UP
│   │   │   └── Put (PE)             — profits when price goes DOWN
│   │   └── Forwards / Swaps         — OTC, mostly institutional
│   ├── Underlyings in India
│   │   ├── Index F&O                — Nifty, Bank Nifty, Fin Nifty, Sensex, etc.
│   │   ├── Stock F&O                — ~200 eligible stocks, monthly expiry
│   │   ├── Commodity F&O            — on MCX
│   │   └── Currency F&O             — USD/INR and other pairs
│   ├── Key terms
│   │   ├── Lot size                 — minimum contract quantity
│   │   ├── Expiry                   — date the contract ends (weekly/monthly)
│   │   ├── Strike price             — the price the option is written at
│   │   ├── Premium                  — price paid for an option
│   │   ├── ITM / ATM / OTM          — in / at / out of the money
│   │   ├── Open interest (OI)       — number of outstanding contracts (positioning)
│   │   └── Rollover                 — move a position to the next expiry
│   ├── Margin
│   │   ├── SPAN + Exposure          — exchange-set margin to hold a position
│   │   └── Peak margin rules        — full margin must be collected upfront
│   └── Reality check
│       └── SEBI studies: ~9 in 10 individual F&O traders lose money — learn before trading
│
├── 06 FIXED INCOME & DEBT  (lend money, earn interest)
│   ├── Government
│   │   ├── G-Secs                   — central government bonds (safest)
│   │   ├── T-Bills                  — short-term government debt (< 1 year)
│   │   ├── SDLs                     — state government bonds
│   │   └── RBI Retail Direct        — buy G-Secs directly as an individual
│   ├── Corporate
│   │   ├── Corporate bonds / NCDs   — higher yield, credit risk
│   │   └── Commercial paper         — short-term corporate borrowing
│   ├── Key concepts
│   │   ├── Yield                    — effective return you earn
│   │   ├── Duration                 — how sensitive price is to rate changes
│   │   ├── Credit rating            — CRISIL / ICRA / CARE grade of default risk
│   │   └── Yield curve              — short vs long rates; inversion = recession warning
│   └── Retail platforms
│       ├── Bond platforms (OBPPs)   — SEBI-registered online bond marketplaces
│       └── Debt mutual funds         — bonds via a fund, easiest route
│
├── 07 MUTUAL FUNDS, ETFs & INDICES  (buy the market, not single stocks)
│   ├── Indices (India)
│   │   ├── Nifty 50                 — top 50 NSE companies, the benchmark
│   │   ├── Sensex                   — top 30 BSE companies
│   │   ├── Bank Nifty               — banking sector, most-traded options index
│   │   ├── Nifty Next 50 / Midcap / Smallcap — broader market slices
│   │   └── India VIX                — expected 30-day volatility ("fear gauge")
│   ├── Mutual fund types
│   │   ├── Equity (large/mid/small/flexi cap) — by company size mandate
│   │   ├── Index funds              — copy an index, lowest cost, no manager bets
│   │   ├── Debt funds               — bonds and money-market instruments
│   │   ├── Hybrid funds             — mix of equity + debt
│   │   └── ELSS                     — tax-saving equity fund, 3-year lock-in
│   ├── Plans & mechanics
│   │   ├── Direct vs Regular        — Direct = no distributor commission, cheaper
│   │   ├── NAV                      — per-unit value, updated daily
│   │   ├── Expense ratio            — annual fee; compounding enemy
│   │   ├── SIP / SWP / STP          — systematic invest / withdraw / transfer
│   │   └── Exit load                — penalty for early redemption
│   ├── ETFs
│   │   ├── Index ETFs               — trade like a stock, track an index
│   │   ├── Gold / Silver ETFs       — precious metals without physical storage
│   │   └── International ETFs       — Nasdaq/S&P exposure (watch the premium to NAV)
│   └── Where to compare
│       ├── Value Research           — fund ratings, portfolios, history
│       ├── Morningstar India        — independent fund research
│       └── AMFI                     — official industry data body
│
├── 08 COMMODITIES & CURRENCY  (beyond company shares)
│   ├── Commodities
│   │   ├── Bullion                  — gold, silver (safe haven, inflation hedge)
│   │   ├── Energy                   — crude oil, natural gas (macro-sensitive)
│   │   ├── Base metals              — copper, zinc, aluminium (economy proxy)
│   │   └── Agri                     — on NCDEX; spices, oilseeds, cotton
│   ├── Currency
│   │   ├── USD/INR                  — most-watched pair for Indian markets
│   │   └── Cross pairs              — EUR/INR, GBP/INR, JPY/INR
│   └── Why it matters to stocks
│       ├── Crude ↑                  — hurts India (net importer), helps upstream oil
│       ├── Rupee ↓                  — helps IT/pharma exporters, hurts importers
│       └── Gold ↑                   — often signals risk-off sentiment
│
├── 09 PRIMARY MARKET  (how companies come to the market)
│   ├── Issue types
│   │   ├── IPO                      — first public share sale
│   │   ├── FPO                      — follow-on issue by an already listed company
│   │   ├── OFS                      — existing holders sell via the exchange
│   │   └── QIP                      — fast raise from institutions only
│   ├── IPO mechanics
│   │   ├── DRHP / RHP               — draft and final offer documents (read these)
│   │   ├── Price band               — the range investors bid within
│   │   ├── Categories               — QIB / NII (HNI) / Retail quotas
│   │   ├── Anchor investors         — institutions locking in a day early
│   │   ├── ASBA / UPI mandate       — money blocked, not debited, until allotment
│   │   └── Listing day              — first day of trading; gain/loss vs issue price
│   └── Signals people watch
│       ├── Subscription multiple    — demand vs shares on offer, by category
│       └── Grey market premium (GMP) — unofficial, unregulated — sentiment only
│
├── 10 ORDERS & TRADE LIFECYCLE  (what happens after you press Buy)
│   ├── Order types
│   │   ├── Market                   — fill now at best available price
│   │   ├── Limit                    — fill only at your price or better
│   │   ├── Stop-loss (SL / SL-M)    — triggers an exit once price hits a level
│   │   ├── GTT                      — standing order valid for up to a year
│   │   ├── Bracket / Cover          — entry + target + stop in one order
│   │   └── AMO                      — placed after hours, sent at next open
│   ├── Product types
│   │   ├── CNC / Delivery           — buy and hold in your demat
│   │   ├── MIS / Intraday           — auto-squared off same day
│   │   ├── NRML                     — carry-forward F&O positions
│   │   └── MTF                      — broker-funded leverage on delivery (interest cost)
│   ├── Market sessions (NSE equity)
│   │   ├── Pre-open                 — 9:00–9:15, sets the opening price
│   │   ├── Normal market            — 9:15–15:30
│   │   └── Closing session          — closing price = weighted avg of last 30 min
│   ├── Settlement
│   │   ├── T+1                      — standard; shares/money credited next day
│   │   └── T+0 (optional)           — same-day settlement in select stocks
│   └── Costs on every trade
│       ├── Brokerage                — broker's fee (flat or %)
│       ├── STT                      — securities transaction tax (higher on F&O)
│       ├── Exchange + SEBI fees     — small turnover charges
│       ├── Stamp duty               — state levy on buy side
│       └── GST                      — on brokerage + exchange charges
│

├── 13 FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS  (what is the business actually worth?)
│   ├── Financial statements
│   │   ├── Income statement (P&L)   — revenue, costs, profit
│   │   ├── Balance sheet            — what it owns vs what it owes
│   │   └── Cash flow statement      — real cash in/out (hardest to fake)
│   ├── Key ratios
│   │   ├── P/E                      — price vs earnings (how expensive)
│   │   ├── P/B                      — price vs book value (banks, asset-heavy)
│   │   ├── EV/EBITDA                — valuation that ignores capital structure
│   │   ├── ROE / ROCE               — how well capital is turned into profit
│   │   ├── Debt/Equity              — leverage risk
│   │   └── PEG                      — P/E adjusted for growth
│   ├── Valuation methods
│   │   ├── DCF                      — present value of future cash flows
│   │   ├── Relative valuation       — compare multiples with peers
│   │   └── Sum-of-the-parts         — value each business separately (conglomerates)
│   ├── Qualitative
│   │   ├── Moat                     — durable competitive advantage
│   │   ├── Management quality       — capital allocation, governance, track record
│   │   ├── Promoter pledging        — pledged shares = red flag
│   │   └── Annual report + concalls — management's own words, read the notes

├── 15 TRADING & INVESTING STYLES  (pick a time horizon first)

│   │   └── Passive / index          — own the market, minimal decisions
│   └── Hybrid approaches

├── 16 OPTIONS STRATEGY & GREEKS  (options are about probability, not direction alone)
│   ├── Pricing
│   │   ├── Intrinsic vs time value  — real value now vs hope value
│   │   ├── Implied volatility (IV)  — market's expected move baked into premium
│   │   ├── IV percentile / rank     — is premium cheap or expensive vs history
│   │   └── Black-Scholes            — the classic theoretical pricing model
│   ├── Greeks
│   │   ├── Delta                    — change in premium per 1-point move
│   │   ├── Gamma                    — how fast delta changes (explodes near expiry)
│   │   ├── Theta                    — daily time decay (sellers' friend)
│   │   ├── Vega                     — sensitivity to IV changes
│   │   └── Rho                      — sensitivity to interest rates (minor)
│   ├── Directional
│   │   ├── Long call / long put     — simple bet, limited loss
│   │   ├── Bull / bear spreads      — capped profit, cheaper, defined risk
│   │   └── Covered call             — earn premium on stock you own
│   ├── Neutral / volatility
│   │   ├── Straddle / strangle      — bet on big move (long) or no move (short)
│   │   ├── Iron condor              — range-bound, defined-risk premium selling
│   │   ├── Butterfly                — pin to a price, cheap, defined risk
│   │   └── Calendar spread          — exploit time-decay difference across expiries
│   ├── Hedging
│   │   ├── Protective put           — insurance on a holding
│   │   └── Collar                   — put funded by selling a call
│   └── Tools
│       ├── Sensibull                — India's leading options strategy builder
│       ├── Opstra                   — payoff diagrams, IV charts, backtests
│       └── Broker option chains     — live Greeks and OI inside your terminal

├── 17 QUANT & ALGORITHMIC TRADING  (rules → code → automated execution)
│   ├── Strategy families

│   │   ├── Mean reversion           — bet that stretched prices snap back
│   │   ├── Statistical arbitrage    — pairs trading, cointegration
│   │   ├── Market making            — quote both sides, earn spread (needs speed)
│   │   ├── Options-selling systems  — systematic premium harvesting with hedges
│   │   └── Factor investing         — value, momentum, quality, low-vol factors
│   ├── The pipeline
│   │   ├── Idea / hypothesis        — why should this edge exist?
│   │   ├── Data cleaning            — adjust for splits, bonuses, survivorship
│   │   ├── Backtest                 — test rules on historical data
│   │   ├── Walk-forward / OOS test  — validate on data the strategy never saw
│   │   ├── Paper trading            — live market, fake money

│   ├── Backtest traps
│   │   ├── Overfitting / curve-fitting — great past, useless future
│   │   ├── Look-ahead bias           — accidentally using future data
│   │   ├── Survivorship bias         — testing only on stocks that still exist
│   │   └── Ignoring costs/slippage   — the silent profit killer
│   ├── Tools & languages

│   │   ├── TradingView Pine Script  — fastest way to test chart ideas
│   │   ├── AmiBroker                — popular with Indian system traders
│   │   └── QuantConnect             — cloud backtesting + live (global markets)
│   ├── Indian algo platforms
│   │   ├── Streak (Zerodha)         — no-code strategy building
│   │   ├── Tradetron                — strategy marketplace + automation
│   │   └── AlgoTest                 — options backtesting and deployment
│   └── Regulation
