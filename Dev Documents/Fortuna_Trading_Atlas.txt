  LEGEND
    — note        what it is / why it matters
    [Author Year] the book or study it comes from (go-to source to read)
    {E+}          strong evidence (peer-reviewed, replicated)
    {E~}          mixed evidence (works in some markets/periods, decays, or
                  works only before costs)
    {E-}          weak or no evidence in studies
    {P}           practitioner knowledge only (widely used, not formally tested)
    ★             already decided / built in Fortuna
    ⇒ F:          where it lives in Fortuna
                  (backend/modules/Module_F_Tech_Indicators_Funnel/<folder>)

  How to use the tags (the Fortuna rule for building rules):
    books first → better study? (the {E} tag) → your own chart observations
    → finalise → code.
  {E-} and {P} items are not thrown away. Store them as low-weight labels,
  because market behaviour changes.
